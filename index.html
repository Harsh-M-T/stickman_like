About

Stickman Fighter is a fast-paced 1v1 brawler. Beat your opponent across multiple rounds; the first to 2 round wins takes the match.

How to Play

Win the Match Defeat your opponent in 2 rounds before they do the same to you. Defend, punish whiffs, and combo for the win.
Difficulty Scales Easy, Normal and Hard tune the enemy AI and stats. Harder enemies hit harder, survive longer, and move faster.
Mix It Up Light punches are fast, heavy punches hit hardest, and sweep kicks catch blocking opponents low. Block cuts damage by 85%.

Tips

  • Block by holding back — it drains a guard meter; guard break leaves you vulnerable.
  • Super is available once the SHAKTI meter is full (built by landing hits and blocking).
  • Heavy punches hit hardest; sweep kicks hit low and knock down.
  • The AI punishes your recovery frames on hard difficulty.
